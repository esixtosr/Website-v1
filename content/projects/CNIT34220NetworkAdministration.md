A Purdue CIT network administration course where I configured Unix-based network services and practiced securing distributed systems with firewalls, service hardening, and secure protocols.

### What this was

CNIT 34220 focused on the installation, administration, and maintenance of distributed computing systems. The course connected network operating systems, interoperability, and service infrastructure through hands-on lab work.

### What I worked on

- Configured and maintained network services such as DNS, mail, web services, and related server components.
- Studied SNMP, mail protocols, proxies, DMZ service placement, and secure network design.
- Practiced securing servers and services with firewall rules, service hardening, and safer protocol choices.
- Documented lab work and troubleshooting steps for network service implementation.

### What I learned

This course helped me understand how network services are built, exposed, monitored, and secured. It connected server administration to the practical security decisions behind websites, mail systems, DMZs, proxies, and distributed infrastructure.
