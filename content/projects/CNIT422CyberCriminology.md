A Purdue CIT course where I studied cybercriminal behavior through criminological, psychological, sociological, and empirical research perspectives.

### What this was

CNIT 42200 focused on cyber criminology and the study of cybercriminal behavior. The course compared traditional and contemporary forms of cybercrime while connecting theory and research to how cyber offenses develop and are understood.

### What I worked on

- Compared traditional criminal behavior with newer forms of cybercriminal activity.
- Applied psychological, sociological, and criminological concepts to cybercrime topics.
- Reviewed empirical literature on theory and cybercriminal behavior.
- Practiced thinking about security problems through human behavior, motivation, and social context.

### What I learned

This course helped me see cybersecurity as more than tools and systems. Understanding attacker behavior, motivation, and social patterns adds useful context to technical defense, policy, investigation, and incident response.
