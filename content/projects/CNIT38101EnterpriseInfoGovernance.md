### What this was

CNIT 38101 was a variable-title Purdue CIT course focused on enterprise information governance. I am keeping this archive entry focused on governance, policy, risk, accountability, and how organizations manage information systems responsibly.

### What I worked on

- Studied enterprise information governance as a bridge between technical systems and organizational policy.
- Reviewed how cybersecurity and systems coursework connect to governance topics.
- Considered how information handling, risk, and accountability affect enterprise environments.
- Built context for later work involving security policy, compliance, and organizational decision-making.

### What I learned

This course fit into the governance side of my cybersecurity path. It helped me understand security and information management as enterprise responsibilities, not only technical tasks handled after systems are already built.
