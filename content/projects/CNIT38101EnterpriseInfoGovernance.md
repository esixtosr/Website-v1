A Purdue CIT variable-title course focused on enterprise information governance and the policy, risk, and organizational context around information systems.

### What this was

CNIT 38101 was listed as a variable-title course used to pilot new course offerings. For my archive, I am treating this entry conservatively as Enterprise Information Governance and keeping the description focused on governance, policy, risk, and enterprise information systems.

### What I worked on

- Studied enterprise information governance as a bridge between technical systems and organizational policy.
- Reviewed how prerequisites in cybersecurity and systems coursework connect to governance topics.
- Considered how information handling, risk, and accountability affect enterprise environments.
- Built context for later work involving security policy, compliance, and organizational decision-making.

### What I learned

This course fit into the governance side of the cybersecurity path. It helped frame security and information management as enterprise responsibilities, not only technical tasks handled after systems are built.
