A Purdue CIT project management course where I studied how IT projects are planned, estimated, staffed, communicated, controlled, and delivered.

### What this was

CNIT 48000 introduced the knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques used to manage information technology projects. The course emphasized scope, risk, budget, time, expectations, quality, people, communications, procurement, and externally provided services.

### What I worked on

- Studied core project management terms, processes, and techniques.
- Practiced thinking through scope, risk, time, budget, and quality tradeoffs.
- Used project management software concepts to plan and manage IT work.
- Applied project management thinking to business and technology problems.

### What I learned

This course helped me understand why technical work needs planning, communication, and risk management. It gave me a better vocabulary for organizing projects and explaining tradeoffs before implementation begins.
