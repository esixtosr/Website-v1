A final-semester Purdue CIT network security course focused on VPNs, firewall rules, authentication, encryption, wireless security, and secure network architecture.

### What this is

CNIT 45500 explores the business, conceptual, and technical parts of securing voice and data networks. The course focuses on analyzing weaknesses, designing secure infrastructure, and applying policy-driven network security controls.

### What I am focusing on

- Deepening my understanding of VPN design, secure remote access, and firewall rule strategy.
- Studying packet filtering, authentication, encryption, wireless security, and security protocols.
- Connecting network security architecture to business requirements and operational constraints.
- Practicing how to evaluate systems, identify weaknesses, and design more secure infrastructure.

### Why it matters

This course ties directly into the kind of network defense work I want to keep building. It strengthens the connection between routing, segmentation, access control, policy, and real-world security architecture.
