An introductory Purdue CIT course where I studied the architectural layers behind information technology systems, including operating systems, network operating systems, distributed systems, and distributed application architectures.

### What this was

CNIT 17600 introduced the major architecture pieces that make IT environments work. The course covered operating systems, network operating systems, distributed systems, distributed applications, interoperability, and current technology trends across those areas.

### What I worked on

- Studied how operating systems, networked systems, and distributed architectures fit together.
- Worked through Raspberry Pi-based labs that connected course concepts to physical hardware.
- Explored how different architecture components interact across devices, services, and applications.
- Built early comfort with hands-on IT experimentation before moving deeper into systems, networking, and security coursework.

### What I learned

This course gave me an early foundation for thinking about IT systems as connected layers rather than isolated tools. It was not one of my strongest cybersecurity turning points, but it helped prepare me for later coursework in system administration, networking, and security.
